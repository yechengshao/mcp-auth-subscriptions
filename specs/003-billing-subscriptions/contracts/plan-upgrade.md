# Contract: Paid Upgrade with Unused-Term Credit

**Status**: User-confirmed new-term upgrade behavior; implementation/live acceptance pending. Manual renewal and one-time Checkout remain unchanged. Active-term self-service downgrade/cancellation/refund is prohibited; all related requests and zero/excess-credit exceptions are routed to the configured support email.

## Quote and calculation

An authenticated currently paid owner selects an approved strictly higher tier, determined by catalog tier_order rather than price or duration, and one offered duration. Billing validates the target catalog/version and current source subscription/revision. Server-side calculation credits the unused value of the old purchased term against the full selected new-term price; the new term does NOT retain the old expiry.

Snapshot source term start/end, current revision, eligible old paid value in integer minor units, quote timestamp and expiry, remaining duration, target tier/duration/catalog version, full target price, credit and final amount. Calculate remaining_fraction=clamp((source_end-quote_time)/(source_end-source_start),0,1); round the resulting unused-value credit down to currency minor units, and subtract it from the full target price. Invalid/zero source duration, currency mismatch or a stale/revoked source denies the quote. Eligible old value must come from settled owned billing records net of applicable previous refunds/consumed credits, never a browser-supplied amount or current catalog price. Exact treatment of taxes/fees and carried historical discounts must be explicit and tested before enabling affected offers; do not overstate financial-policy completeness.

Quote expiry is a bounded deployment input and must be shown with the full target price, credit, payable amount, selected duration and new-term rule before confirmation. A changed source revision or expired quote requires a fresh confirmed quote; never silently change the charge. No self-service downgrade/cancellation, negative Stripe charge, automatic refund or cash payout is authorized; rejected requests retain the current entitlement unchanged. If credit is at least target price, return unsupported_credit_case with the configured support contact and no Checkout creation, rather than silently losing or paying out the excess.

## Checkout and activation

After explicit quote confirmation, conditionally claim an upgrade intent against that source revision so concurrent tabs cannot consume the same credit twice. Persist quote/effect intent before creating one-time Checkout. Use server-derived price_data for the payable integer amount and approved target product/currency; retain the original catalog price reference and credit breakdown in the order. This is an authorized upgrade-only net-price path, not arbitrary browser pricing or a global reusable discount code. Provider payment verification must compare the resulting amount/currency/ownership against the immutable upgrade quote, not blindly require the full undiscounted catalog amount.

Keep old entitlement and its native usage-plan association unchanged while payment is pending, cancelled, failed or uncertain. Reuse pending Checkout on retry, with stable provider idempotency and durable credit reservation. Browser return never applies the upgrade; the signed webhook or reconciliation verifies payment. Expired/uncertain Checkout cannot release its reservation until billing proves no payable session can still succeed; preserve recovery intent rather than issuing competing quotes/charges.

On successful verified payment, atomically consume the credit once, record payment/history, supersede the old application term and publish the new entitlement revision and purchased mapping snapshot. The new term starts at the verified successful-payment timestamp, and expires after the newly selected duration under its snapshotted term rule; webhook delay does not move that timestamp. Late/conflicting payment after an intervening source change is a paid repair conflict, not an automatic overwrite or repeated charge.

Access's next authorized onboarding/management request reuses the subscriber API key, removes the old stage association and adds the new target usage plan as required, then conditionally saves the current revision after successful AWS responses under the REST success rule. Do not perform a propagation probe. AWS errors show retry/support guidance, preserve the paid upgrade and reconcile uncertain effects idempotently. Do not undo payment merely because provisioning is incomplete. Existing JWTs use the same key and retain their original expiry; changes to native association can independently affect their metering. Issue new credentials only against current entitlement/binding, with the existing token/refresh bounds.

## Acceptance

Test remaining-time rounding, nearly-expired term, past catalog discounts/refunds, unsupported currency/zero-or-negative payable, quote expiry/revision change, explicit amount confirmation, concurrent credit consumption, duplicate/late events, failed payment preserving old access, crash after provider effects, delayed success starting the correct new duration and repeated native association failure without repurchase. B003/B005/B007/B009/B012 own quote/payment/reducer/management integration; access T017/T018/T020 own request-driven reassociation and credential gates. No production charges or refunds occur from this specification.
